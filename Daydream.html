Autoplay

With this many seconds left, the next song fades in while the current one fades out. Set to 0 to turn it off. Applies when tracks change automatically.

Browsers only allow sound after you've clicked something on the page, so autoplay begins once you interact.

Slowed + reverb

Turn it on with the Slowed + Reverb button at the bottom, or press R. Speed also lowers the pitch, like the classic edits.

A profile saves your colors, visualizer, EQ, autoplay and crossfade on this computer. Exporting to a file also includes the background image and lets you carry everything over to another computer.
